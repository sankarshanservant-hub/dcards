Terms of Participation for Authors in the Dialogue Cards Project

Version 1.0 of {{DATE}}

1. General provisions

This agreement sets out the terms of participation of authors in the Dialogue Cards project ("Cards of Dialogue") and the procedure for using the images they provide.

Organisational matters related to the participation of authors and the use of their works are decided by the administration of the Dialogue Cards project (hereinafter, the Project).

The Dialogue Cards collection is created by different authors, each of whom brings their own experience, their own images and their view of life. The cards are intended for communication, reflection, people getting to know each other, holding Dialogue Flow meetings and other forms of interaction with the cards provided for by the Project.

2. Submitting works and participating in the collection

The author reads this agreement and the guide for illustrators, uploads an image and sends it for curatorial review.

The author permits the Project to store the uploaded image, to carry out the necessary technical processing and to give curators access to it for review.

Following the curatorial selection, the work may be accepted into the collection, sent back for revision or declined.

Sending a work does not guarantee its inclusion in the collection.

The author may propose an updated version of their work, which also goes through curatorial selection. Until the new version is accepted, the previous one remains in the collection, unless the author has withdrawn permission to use it.

Works that are finally declined are not published, are not used in the Project's materials and are subject to deletion.

3. Copyright

The author retains all rights they hold in the image and may freely use it outside the Project.

When a work is accepted into the collection, the author grants the Project administration a non-exclusive, royalty-free permission to use the specific accepted version of the image in the ways provided for by this agreement.

The permission applies to the territory of all countries of the world and is granted for the entire term of the exclusive right to the image, if such a right exists, with the possibility of early termination at the author's initiative.

The permission does not require periodic renewal and does not involve the transfer of exclusive rights to the Project.

4. Use of images

The author permits the work accepted into the collection to be used:

— in the digital library, on the website and in the Project's applications;
— in games and other interactive formats of the Project;
— at online and offline Dialogue Flow meetings;
— in the Project's methodological, educational and informational materials;
— on the Project's social media and in its presentation materials;
— in advertising materials of Dialogue Cards and Dialogue Flow, including paid advertising;
— in the formats provided for by the Project for exchanging cards between users, including digital postcards.

For these purposes the Project may reproduce the images, display them publicly, place them on the internet, provide access to them and distribute them as part of the materials provided for by this agreement.

The author consents to facilitators and users being granted limited non-exclusive rights to use the images within the relevant formats and rules of the Project.

Facilitators may use the cards at free and paid Dialogue Flow meetings, and may themselves print the images available in the gallery for holding such meetings, without the right to sell the printed cards.

The Project may engage the technical services and platforms necessary for storing, displaying and distributing the images and for promoting the Project, solely within the purposes permitted by this agreement.

Facilitators and users are not granted the right to sell the images or copies of them, to claim authorship, to transfer rights to the images to other persons or to include the images in their own products outside the formats provided for by the Project.

All the ways of use provided for by this agreement are royalty-free and do not require additional approval from the author or payment of remuneration to the author, including when paid meetings are held and paid advertising is placed.

Use of the images in ways not provided for by this agreement requires the author's separate consent.

5. Attribution

When a work is published in the digital library, the name of the author or a pseudonym chosen by the author is always indicated.

On social media and in other materials of the Project, authorship is indicated where possible, taking into account the format of the publication.

The author agrees that their name may not be shown directly on card thumbnails, in game interfaces, digital postcards, advertising and other materials of the Project, if this is due to the format of their use and is permitted by applicable law.

The absence of the author's name in particular materials does not mean a waiver of authorship or a transfer of the right of authorship to another person.

6. Technical processing of images

The Project may carry out the technical processing of images necessary for their use: changing the size, resolution and format of files, compressing images, creating thumbnails, adapting images for interfaces, digital postcards and other materials of the Project.

Such processing must not substantially change the artistic content of the work.

Substantial changes to the illustration itself require agreement with the author.

7. Respect for the rights of third parties

The author confirms that they hold the necessary rights to the image provided and have lawful grounds to permit its use for the purposes provided for by this agreement.

When creating a work using third-party materials, digital tools or artificial intelligence, the author undertakes to respect the rights of third parties and the terms of use of the relevant services.

If justified claims from third parties arise, the Project may temporarily suspend the use of the image until the circumstances are clarified.

Each party is responsible for its own violations in accordance with applicable law.

8. Removal of a work and termination of its use

The author may at any time withdraw the permission granted, remove their work from the collection or withdraw it from curatorial review through their personal account or by contacting the Project administration.

From the moment the request is received, the Project stops further use of the image and the granting of new permissions to use it.

The card and the materials related to it are deleted from all resources controlled by the Project no later than 30 calendar days from the moment the request is received.

Technical and archive copies may be kept temporarily if this is necessary for the operation of the system or for compliance with legal requirements. Such copies are not used publicly and are deleted at the end of the necessary retention period.

Permissions granted to facilitators and users for further use of the image within the Project end when the author withdraws permission or when the card is finally excluded from the collection.

The Project notifies facilitators of the removal of a card. Facilitators must stop using it at subsequent meetings, including in printed form.

The Project cannot guarantee the deletion of copies of the image saved, printed or obtained by third parties earlier that are outside its control.

The Project may also exclude a work from the collection on its own initiative, notifying the author.

9. Acceptance of the agreement

The author accepts this agreement electronically when submitting a work through the Project's website.

By accepting the agreement, the author confirms that they have the legal capacity necessary to conclude it on their own and to grant the rights provided for by it.

If the author's participation requires the consent of a legal representative, the author must contact the Project administration in advance.

The fact of acceptance of the agreement is recorded in the Project's system, indicating the author, the specific work, the version of the image, the version of the agreement and the date of confirmation.

10. Changes to the terms and final provisions

When the agreement is changed, the new version is published on the Project's website.

Changes that extend the rights granted to the Project or establish new obligations for the author apply to previously uploaded works only after the author's consent has been obtained.

If the legal structure of the Project changes, relations with authors are formalised in accordance with applicable law and with the necessary consents obtained.

Any disagreements are resolved first of all through negotiation.

Relations under this agreement are governed by the law of the Russian Federation, taking into account the mandatory rules of other law where they apply.

On questions of participation in the collection, use of the images and termination of cooperation, the author may contact the Dialogue Cards administration through the contact details provided on the Project's website.
